Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to list an event?

Listing your event is absolutely free. We only charge a small convenience fee on successful ticket sales.

When do I receive the money from ticket sales?

Payouts are processed 3-5 business days after your event has successfully concluded.

Can I cancel or postpone my event?

Yes, you have full control via your dashboard. In case of cancellation, we handle the refund process for the customers automatically.

Yes, for events with over 500 attendees, we can provide turnstiles, handheld scanners, and support personnel.
