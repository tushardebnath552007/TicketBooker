Frequently Asked Questions regarding Offers

Q1: How do I apply a credit card discount on CineDeals?

A: Once you select your movie seats, navigate to the payment page. Under the "Unlock Offers or Apply Promocodes" section, select 'Credit Card', enter your card numbering parameters, and click apply.

Q2: Can I stack two wallet or bank offers together?

A: Generally, promotional regulations state that you can only apply one promotional code or banking discount card offer per individual transaction tracking sequence.

Q3: Why isn't my Buy 1 Get 1 Free offer going through?

A: Most bank partnerships have daily allocation quotas. If the quota for the day is completed, the system will prevent the application until quotas refresh the following morning.
